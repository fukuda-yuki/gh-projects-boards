Reviewed against AGENTS.md, DESIGN.md, tests/README.md and the current Issue 65 assignment, with installed WinUI development, design, UI-testing and code-review guidance. It does not assert a particular private template tree, call count or the presence/absence of scrollbars. Existing real-control and ordinary-input tests retain the broader contracts.
